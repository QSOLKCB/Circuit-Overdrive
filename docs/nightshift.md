# After Dark / Nightshift

Choose a course, select **Nightshift** and check the lit scene behind the menu. Start race brings the six-car field to the grid. Press W or ↑ to arm with the keyboard, press Confirm with a neutral controller or tap **Drive with touch**. The countdown begins on that input.

| Standalone event | Route | Race format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nightshift Speedway | Overdrive Speedway | Three banked laps |
| Redline Ridge — Midnight Descent | Redline Ridge | Point-to-point mountain sprint |

The event title appears above the map and on the pause and finish panels. Use C to switch chase cameras and Esc to pause. Restart keeps the chosen lighting. To return to daylight, open Course select and choose **Day**. The championship runs its inaugural four-event calendar in daylight.

Both night events play **Midnight Protocol**, the original 144 BPM dark chiptune. Settings → Sound mix adjusts music, engine and effects independently. The master switch mutes the entire mix. The first driving gesture activates audio.

Settings → Driving effects offers **Reduced effects** and a lower-resolution **Mobile** option. Auto follows the device and reduced-motion preference. The road and course boundaries are shared by all quality levels.

A finished night race saves a command tape with `-night-replay.json` in the filename. In Replay Theatre, open the local file, then choose **Nightshift** under Spectator lighting to see the course at night again. Theatre starts in daylight on each import and lets you change lighting, follow any competitor and seek in either direction. [Replay Theatre guide](replay-theatre.md)
