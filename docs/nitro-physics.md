# CO-009A · Nitro driving authority

Nitro is a separate 60 Hz discipline for finite sprint courses. Each of the six drivers supplies a fresh record with `throttle` and `brake` in [0,1], `steer` in [−1,1], and Boolean `drift` and `boost`. Admission requires all five own data properties and finite analog values. The race validates the player's input before advancing the countdown, AI or physics. Recovery remains a separate accepted request in the tick recording.

VANILLAJS vehicle3d revision `9d95d74b355ec17add32db0b55f0f71130f9c004` integrates four 1/240 s substeps per command. Nitro uses its original gravity, suspension, tyres, inertia, orientation and collision state. The game reuses the touring chassis, original pedal/steering map and speed-dependent suspension load. The race integrates every driver from the same tick-start field, resolves the existing swept car contacts in driver-ID order, then computes gate credit, charge and landing events.

## Drift and charge

Holding drift above 10 m/s on forward, supported asphalt selects a second immutable tyre recipe. Grip changes from 1.55 to 1.10, and cornering stiffness changes from 5,000 to 2,500 N per m/s of lateral slip. A bounded yaw couple tends toward the signed steering request times at most 0.80 rad/s around the road normal. Its angular acceleration limit is 1.9 rad/s². The vehicle solver integrates the resulting angular velocity, suspension loads, lateral forces and chassis orientation. Releasing drift restores the touring tyres.

Energy is an integer from 0 to 100,000 thousandths of one dashboard unit. A qualifying slide requires three loaded asphalt wheels, over 150 N apiece, a forward chassis speed of at least 10 m/s, sideslip between 0.032 and 0.67 radians, and at least one loaded wheel with measured lateral slip speed of 0.65 m/s. The car must advance over 0.08 m past its furthest station each tick and remain a metre inside the road edge. Eight qualifying ticks and a steering demand of at least 0.14 admit credit. The earn rate is 95–535 thousandths per tick, scaled by sideslip and measured tyre motion. Changing steering direction locks charging for roughly a quarter second. A car or rail strike locks it for roughly half a second. Reverse travel, missed gates, recovery and ungrounded motion interrupt the slide. The frontier survives recovery so driving a previously covered sector again earns no charge.

## Boost and flight

Boost requires energy, positive throttle, forward gear and speed, supported asphalt and a released brake. It supplies 11 m/s² along the real chassis forward axis for the accepted fraction of the 1/60 s step. At full delivery it consumes 440 thousandths per tick (26.4 units/s). A partial final tick spends exactly the remaining energy. Headroom below 68 m/s in total velocity also limits the applied impulse. This leaves 52 m/s of margin below the engine's 120 m/s safety limit. Tyres, grade, drag, contacts and brakes act on the resulting motion. Boost supplies zero thrust in flight.

Takeoff is the transition from physical wheel support to zero grounded wheels. While airborne, holding drift enables bounded attitude control. Throttle and brake request pitch, steering requests yaw and bank. Each chassis-axis angular acceleration is limited to 1 rad/s² toward pitch/yaw/roll targets of at most 0.65/0.60/0.50 rad/s. The game applies angular impulses before the normal gravity and angular integration. Translational velocity receives no attitude-control impulse.

The first wheel touching the road ends the airborne indication. Three ticks with at least two loaded asphalt wheels confirm a supported landing. The race records the takeoff station, ramp identity, duration, peak clearance and last airborne vertical speed. These observations supply the course's one-shot stunt rules. Racing gates require a road-supported chassis and the original swept forward crossing and vertical test. Recovery includes the established 180-tick penalty and selects a clear slot at or behind the latest credited gate. It clears energy and pending flight credit.

## Physical checks

Run `node --test tests/nitro-physics.test.mjs`. On the analytic suspension test bed in Node 22.18, seed 19, a 50-tick steering input of 0.65 following 190 acceleration ticks earned 5.5 units. Advancing the same physical state with and without boost increases forward velocity by over 0.1 m/s in the boosted step. The regression checks depletion, braking, stationary and steering-oscillation inputs, barrier contact, airborne gate rejection and recovery. Its full-speed ramp drive measures loss of all four contacts, downward acceleration on successive flight ticks, a rising then falling trajectory, and a loaded landing.

Each car uses four suspension contacts on a sampled height surface; chassis-to-chassis contacts use a swept horizontal footprint restricted to overlapping heights. At a rail the lowest point of the rotated airborne chassis determines clearance above its top. A large excursion outside the course, an overturned chassis or a solver recovery flag invokes the normal penalised recovery. This simplified model supports ground ramps and airborne travel under downward gravity.
