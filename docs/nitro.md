# Nitro Overdrive

Select **Nitro Overdrive**, then **Start race**. Take the wheel on the six-car grid and accelerate when the lights go green. Nitro Skyway crosses three launch decks above an industrial basin. Six checkpoints lead to the 1.92 km flag.

Cross each numbered gate in order. The full lane between the guardrails counts, including the paved apron. After all six checkpoints, crossing the chequered line on your wheels settles your result immediately, even when you lead the field. If you fly over a gate or miss it, the HUD names it. Use **Recover** (**R** on keyboard) to return to a credited stretch and retry with the three-second penalty.

| Action | Keyboard | Standard gamepad | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Drift | Hold Space and steer into the bend | Hold left bumper and steer | Hold Drift and steer |
| Boost | Hold Shift while accelerating | Right bumper and accelerator | Boost and accelerator |
| Adjust attitude in flight | Hold Space, use pedals for pitch and steering for yaw and roll | Hold Drift and use pedals and stick | Hold Drift and use pedal and steering buttons |

Carry speed into a sweeping corner. As the rear starts sliding, balance steering and throttle. The meter shows **Drift charging** when the tyres carry load and the car makes forward progress with controlled sideslip. Straighten for the exit and apply boost. Braking takes priority. The 100-unit meter starts empty on every grid. A recovery costs three seconds and clears stored energy.

Line up with the centre of a launch deck before its crest. The suspension unloads as the road falls away. Let the car settle on the marked landing section and continue through the next gate. A qualifying jump pays stunt points once when its landing is accepted. Race position, checkpoint order and official time decide the result.

**Nitro Pulse** is an original 160 BPM score. Adjust Music and Effects separately in Settings. The reduced effects setting suppresses exhaust and landing particles and limits camera movement.
