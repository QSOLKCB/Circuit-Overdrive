# Rocket Overdrive

Select **Rocket Overdrive** on the course screen, start the race and arm the six-car grid. Orbital Spine is a point-to-point sprint. The timing gates run across the road, including the vertical and inverted spans. Each driver gets an official finish time.

**Accelerate and steer through the wall and ceiling.** The magnetic road pulls the car toward the surface under its wheels automatically. You can lift, brake to a stop upside down and accelerate away with an empty boost tank. Steer relative to the car as the chase camera rolls with the road. The HUD shows the current surface, grip and next gate. C switches to a higher chase view.

On the opening straight, accelerate and steer normally through the grid line and the first numbered gate. If the car comes to rest on its side or roof, the HUD shows the recovery and the race returns it to a safe deck after 1.5 seconds, with the usual three-second penalty. You can also use Recover after a head-on collision.

At the open jump, line up with the catch deck and keep driving. When the road ends, the wheels release and gravity carries the car through an arc. Landing assistance steadies the car as its wheels approach the catch deck. You can clear the jump on the accelerator alone. In flight, steering trims yaw and roll, and the pedals trim pitch. Shift adds forward thrust while fuel lasts. Q and E spend fuel for a left or right air dodge. Release the dodge and wait for the cooldown before the next one. [Controller guide](controllers.md)

R requests a safe return to a previously credited upright deck. The race adds three seconds to the driver's time. The entire field uses the same four-wheel suspension and contact rules. A side-by-side pass on the wall can produce a real impact. The championship keeps its original four events.

The reduced-effects setting removes boost plumes and impact flashes and fixes the camera field of view. The oblique course map shows the elevated return span. The traffic inset uses the driver's local right and forward axes, including on the ceiling. [Course geometry](orbital-spine.md) · [Dynamics](rocket-physics.md)

RASTER VELOCITY runs at 180 BPM on the existing music mixer. At the flag, select **Save replay**, open **Replay Theatre** on the same source build and load the v4 file. Select any of the six racers and seek to a wall or ceiling split. [Recording format, limits and source compatibility](replay-theatre.md)
