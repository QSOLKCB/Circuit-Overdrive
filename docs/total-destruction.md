# Total Destruction

Total Destruction is the v1.2.0 candidate's six-car derby. Each car uses the same 60 Hz collision and damage authority. Your rivals steer, accelerate, brake and reverse through the same command path you use.

## Start a round

1. Choose **Total Destruction** from Circuit Overdrive's main menu.
2. Select **The Crucible** for a broad industrial crash pit or **Iron Pit** for tighter passes around guarded fixtures.
3. Choose **Arm grid**. The game locks the arena and displays the six cars on their starting marks.
4. Choose **Start round** or press **W** or **Up** from the keyboard-focused grid. Drive when the countdown clears.

| Action | Keyboard |
| --- | --- |
| Accelerate | W or Up |
| Brake, then reverse | S or Down |
| Steer | A / D or Left / Right |
| Cycle camera | C |
| Pause or resume | Esc |

Use **Controller** to select touch controls, a standard gamepad or a saved profile. Release steering, pedals and buttons before arming a controller. Gamepad confirm activates the focused control. Confirming on the arena canvas starts an armed round or resumes a paused one. Tabbing out of the arena while driving with the keyboard pauses the round and clears held keys. Changing input, leaving the window or losing graphics also pauses a live match. Resume with fresh inputs once focus and the selected device are ready.

## Read the arena

The HUD displays accepted time remaining, operational cars, your credited knockouts, the nearest operational rival and the latest accepted impact. The body diagram shows accumulated front, rear, left and right damage. The component bars show remaining engine, steering, tyre and suspension condition. Move around rivals or aim for an exposed side when direct approaches leave your engine vulnerable.

A car whose engine or tyres reach full damage loses propulsion. Wrecks stay solid and can still roll and be struck. The standings show their elimination and credited knockouts. A wall-caused mechanical failure receives no attacker credit.

When your car is eliminated, the camera follows an operational rival. Use **Spectator follow** and the driver selector to inspect any of the six cars. The round continues to an official result. The winner display identifies the last operational driver or the top survivor on the accepted time-limit classification. Simultaneous final failures can leave no winner. A technical stop holds the last displayed valid state and identifies the stop before offering a restart.

## Camera and audio

**Chase** follows your chassis. **Raised arena** frames the enclosed field. **Spectator follow** shows the selected car from a higher trailing angle. The presentation respects the saved driving-effects quality and system reduced-motion preference.

**STEEL RIOT** plays at 150 BPM after you start the round. The shared mixer lets you adjust music, engine, effects and master levels for this session. Crash cues use accepted impulse and body region. Failure and victory cues use accepted elimination and result events.
